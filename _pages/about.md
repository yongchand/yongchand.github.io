Hi, I'm a PhD student in the Department of Quantitative and Computational Biology at the [University of Southern California](https://usc.edu/), advised by [Vsevolod Katritch](https://dornsife.usc.edu/profile/vsevolod-katritch/) and [Yan Liu](https://sites.google.com/view/yanliu-ai/home). I am currently also working with the [Ellison Medical Institute](https://ellisonmedicalinstitute.org/) as a visiting student researcher. Prior to my PhD, I obtained my bachelor's degree in Mathematical and Computational Biology at [Harvey Mudd College](https://www.hmc.edu/). Between my bachelor's studies, I returned to Korea to serve my mandatory military service and worked as a software engineer at various companies, including [Krust Universe](https://krustuniverse.com/) and [Krafton](https://www.krafton.com/en/games/battlegrounds/).

Research
======
My research focuses on building **"reliable" computational drug discovery** along three directions:

1. Creating **reliable benchmarks and training datasets**
2. Integrating **physics and chemistry information** inside models
3. Using **uncertainty quantification** and **Bayesian optimization**

I'm always happy to chat with anyone interested in the computational drug discovery field — please reach out!
